Skiers were getting hurt more and more often on the mountain. Wy'east president Everett Darr and Barney Macnab, captain of the Nile River Yacht Club, persuaded the U.S. Forest Service to do something about it. The Mount Hood Ski Patrol dates its beginning to 1937, when Wy'easter Hank Lewis became the first patroller on the mountain.
