A group of young Portland men, several of them already Mazamas or members of the Trails Club of Oregon, wanted a smaller and more committed circle of climbing partners. They were drawn to unclimbed terrain on Mount Hood.

**Sources disagree on the year.** Conrad's book and Jack Grauer's history both give December 1930. A PNWSAR history gives 1931 and adds a memorable detail: the club organized after a hike up the Larch Mountain Trail from Multnomah Falls, holding its first meeting "in the men's restroom of the Multnomah Falls Lodge."
