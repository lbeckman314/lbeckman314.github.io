Conrad credits the Wy'east Climbers with the first ascent of the steep, crumbling Eliot Glacier Headwall on Hood's north side. The party is given as Russ McJury and Joe Leuthold.

**Sources disagree on the year.** Two sources give 1938, and Mountain Project gives 1935.
