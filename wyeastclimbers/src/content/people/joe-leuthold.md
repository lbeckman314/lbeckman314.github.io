Mount Hood's **Leuthold Couloir** carries Joe Leuthold's name. Secondary sources pair him with Russ McJury on the first ascents of the **Sandy Glacier Headwall** (1937) and the **Eliot Glacier Headwall** (usually given as 1938).

Leuthold also took photographs. A 1936 picture of him with Ranger the climbing dog, near the future site of Timberline Lodge, is credited to the Mazama Library & Historical Collections.
