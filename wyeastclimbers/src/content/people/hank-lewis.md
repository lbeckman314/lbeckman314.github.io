The Mount Hood Ski Patrol dates its beginning to 1937, when Hank Lewis, a Wy'east Climber (national patrol number 78), became the first patroller on the mountain. When the patrol incorporated on March 2, 1938, he became its patrol chief.

Decades later Lewis was one of Ric Conrad's key interviewees and was "instrumental," in Conrad's words, in reconstructing the club's rescue and recovery operations.
