Russ McJury was one of the surviving Wy'easters Ric Conrad interviewed for his book. He is credited with four first ascents on Mount Hood:

- the **North Face**, with Bill Hackett (1936)
- the **Sandy Glacier Headwall**, with Joe Leuthold (1937)
- the **Eliot Glacier Headwall**, with Joe Leuthold (1938)
- **Castle Crags**, with Dave Young and Dave Wagstaff (1951)
