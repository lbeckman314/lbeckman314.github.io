Conrad calls climbers who belonged to none of the organized clubs (not the Mazamas, not the Crag Rats, not the Wy'east Climbers) the "outlaws." Some of them made first ascents or speed ascents, and they often roped up with Wy'easters. Conrad names Gary Leech, Bill "Smoke" Blanchard and Hubert North.

*These three could later have profiles of their own.*
