Everett Darr was president of the Wy'east Climbers when, together with Barney Macnab of the Nile River Yacht Club, he pressed the U.S. Forest Service to look after the growing number of injured skiers on Mount Hood. That push led to the Mount Hood Ski Patrol, where Darr held national patrol number 18.

His American Alpine Journal obituary credits him, with James Mount and Ralph Calkin, with the first climb of the **Wy'east Route** on Mount Hood.

In 1937 Everett and his wife **Ida Darr** started the Mountain Shop in Portland. It later moved to Government Camp, and the couple went on to run the Skibowl, Summit and Multorpor ski areas. Younger climbers and skiers knew him as "Papa Darr." He died on June 21, 1981, at 74.
