Ralph Calkin is credited with Everett Darr and James Mount on the first climb of Mount Hood's **Wy'east Route**. He carried Ranger the climbing dog to his grave below the summit in July 1940. In 1946 he and Jim Harlow went back to fix a bronze plaque to the cairn.

*Note:* sources spell his name both "Calkin" and "Calkins".
